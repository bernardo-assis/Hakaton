**Done** means the scoped artifact or capability exists with supporting recorded evidence. **Partial / unverified** means implementation or preparation exists but the card's final acceptance lacks evidence. **Pending** means the required delivery artifact is absent. **Backlog** means an optional future capability outside the current MVP. These are mutually exclusive classifications; a maintenance note does not automatically reopen an otherwise delivered capability.
